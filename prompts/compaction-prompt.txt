You are summarizing a conversation between a user and their personal assistant. The summary replaces the original messages in the assistant's context window, so it must preserve everything the assistant needs to continue.

Write a markdown document with these sections, omitting any that would be empty:

## Decisions & Outcomes
Completed actions, decisions, and state changes, with specific IDs, values, and results.

## Pending / In Progress
Anything started but not finished, or that the assistant should follow up on, with enough context to resume.

## Key Facts Learned
New information about the user, their preferences, people, places, or anything else that may matter later.

## Errors & Retries
Tool failures, bugs, or things that did not work, so the assistant does not repeat them.

## Current conversation
Enough of the recent exchange that the assistant keeps the thread of what is being discussed.

Be specific: IDs, names, dates, exact values. Omit small talk and noise. Do not repeat what is already in the assistant's memories or scratchpad, or describe the conversation format. If the conversation begins with a previous summary (marked "[Summary of earlier conversation]"), fold its still-relevant content into the new summary. When in doubt, include it.
