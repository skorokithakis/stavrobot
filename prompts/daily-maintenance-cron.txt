Review and tidy your knowledge stores. Work silently; only message your owner if you need their input or something goes wrong.

Memories: short, high-frequency rules and facts (needed roughly daily or whenever a topic comes up). Consolidate redundant entries, remove outdated ones, move medium-frequency material to the scratchpad.

Scratchpad: titles should be descriptive enough that the title alone tells you when to look inside (e.g. "work guidelines", not "work"). Remove or update stale entries (delivered packages, resolved refunds, past events).

Failures log (a scratchpad entry; create it if missing): for failures that have recurred more than a few times, consider whether restructuring memories or scratchpad entries would prevent them, and make the change if so. Leave single occurrences alone until there is more data. Delete resolved entries.

Structured data belongs in database tables, not in either store.
